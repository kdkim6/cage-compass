How this tool reaches its conclusions

Scores run from 0 (less observable distance) to 100 (greater observable distance). The four CAGE scores remain visible: the weighted index is a planning summary, not a standard CAGE score or a prediction of market success. Low: below 35; moderate: 35–64.9; high: 65 or more. These are transparent, illustrative bands, not validated cutoffs.

  • Cultural: 70% of the mean absolute difference across mutually available Hofstede cultural indicators, plus 30% official-language dissimilarity. If Hofstede data are unavailable, the score is a language-only proxy and is flagged as limited.
  • Administrative: When a qualifying trade arrangement is documented, 60% of the World Bank Regulatory Quality score difference plus 40% agreement friction (fixed 15/100). Otherwise only the governance gap is scored, with the trade-agreement status labeled unverified. Sanctions are not automatically cleared; the U.S.–Mexico example documents OFAC's program index but still requires party-level screening.
  • Geographic: 55% capital-to-capital great-circle distance (capped at 8,000 km), 25% presence of a shared land border (0 if yes; 70 if no), and 20% relative difference in World Bank 2023 logistics scores. Missing logistics scores are excluded and remaining weights rescaled.
  • Economic: absolute log ratio of World Bank GDP per capita in current U.S. dollars; an eightfold gap maps to 100. GDP per capita approximates spending power but does not measure household disposable income, within-country inequality, or restaurant-level purchasing behavior.

Before acting: Verify tariff treatment, investment and sector rules, sanctions, local labor and food regulations, demand, unit economics, and market-specific delivery routes. The tool cannot replace legal advice or a city-level business case. Sources are linked in each dimension and included in the PDF.

Sources: Geert Hofstede data · World Bank WDI · World Bank WGI · World Bank LPI · WTO RTA database · OFAC sanctions.
